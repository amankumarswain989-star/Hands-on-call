Flexible Working Hours

Hands On Call provides workers according to your day-to-day requirements, with charges based on the number of hours you need. Customers can choose from 1 hour, 3 hours, 5 hours, or 8 hours (1 day) of service. If you need the worker for additional hours beyond your selected duration, you can simply contact Hands On Call, and we will try to arrange the extra working hours according to the worker's availability and your requirements.
